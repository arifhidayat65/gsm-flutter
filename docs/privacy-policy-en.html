Privacy Policy — e‑Pajak

Last updated: 2025-01-01

The e‑Pajak app helps you check vehicle tax information. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This document explains what data the app processes and how it is used.

Summary

  • No collection of sensitive personal data.
  • Internet access is required to retrieve tax data from third‑party services.
  • PDF generation/sharing happens on your device and is only shared when you choose a location or target app.
  • No ads.

Data Processed

  • User input: license plate number and region selection. This is sent to tax checking services to obtain results and is not stored by the developer.
  • Result data: tax summary displayed to you and optionally exported to PDF.

Storage & Sharing

  • PDF files: created locally on the device. You can save or share via the system save/share dialog. The app does not upload PDFs to any server.

App Permissions

  • Internet: to load tax data.
  • No legacy storage permissions (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE); saving/sharing uses the OS secure mechanisms.

Third Parties

Libraries used (e.g., PDF generation/preview) run on-device and do not send data without your action.

Security

  • Network communication uses HTTPS when supported by the destination service.
  • Do not share PDFs with untrusted parties.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact: support@your-domain.com.
